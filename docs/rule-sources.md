## Expanded semantic test catalog

The controlled recognition catalog now contains 24 candidate records, including the original Japan railway-crossing and Philippines no-right-turn records. Japan's five country-specific test categories and all Japanese shared categories are grounded in the [National Police Agency Traffic Lights and Road Signs](https://www.npa.go.jp/english/bureau/traffic/traffic-light_english.pdf) and [NPA road-sign traveler sheet](https://www.npa.go.jp/english/bureau/traffic/document/r8_followtherules_en02.pdf). The Philippines five country-specific categories and shared categories are grounded in the [LTO Filipino Driver's Manual, Volume 1](https://lto.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/FDM-Vol.-1-2nd-Edition.pdf).

The five Japan-specific test categories are Slow, Use of the Horn, moped two-stage right turn, Priority Road Ahead, and road closed without tire chains. The five Philippines-specific categories are No Jeepneys, No Tricycles, No Pushcarts, No Animal-drawn Vehicles, and BUS–PUJ Stop. Shared normalized categories are Stop, No Entry, Maximum Speed, Pedestrian Crossing, No Parking, and No U-turn.

The illustrations under `frontend/public/signs/test/` are original, simplified test fixtures generated by `scripts/generate-sign-fixtures.mjs` from the catalog descriptions. They support repeatable model testing and do not claim exact official plate geometry. They are not training images or pixel templates.

The rule text in `shared/rules/rules.json` is a paraphrase of these sources. It is not a verbatim quotation of a statute. No fixed distance, penalty, or location-specific exception is inferred from an image. Groq-hosted Qwen identifies an allowlisted candidate sign but is never a rule source.

## Unverified claims kept out of guidance

Current Makati number-coding hours, exceptions, and boundary geometry are not established here. The route preview stays labeled simulation. A universal ban on sandals or slippers for every driver or rider is not established by the sources in issue #3; the UI may only give a parked gear reminder without a legal claim.

## Gate to `tested`

Run the integrated stationary test in `docs/test-matrix.md` with a physical sign through the live camera, then record the observed model ID, source-linked rule, speech output, negative/unknown controls, browser, date, and failures. Ranee reviews that evidence before a record is promoted from `candidate` to `tested`.
